HR INITIATIVES
CORE-TIET in its mission to generate quality human resources in the area of Agro and Industrial Biotechnology for education, research as well as for industry offers short term certificate courses.  These courses are tailored as per the profile of participant’s ie whether they are students, entrepreneurs or industry personnel.  The various short term courses which are offered by CORE-TIET are;
Microbial Biotechnology
Plant Tissue Culture and Plant Biotechnology
Molecular Medicine and Diagnostics
Analytical Techniques in Biotechnology
In vitro techniques in Drug Discovery
Computational Biology and in silico methods of drug discovery/design
Till date CORE-TIET has conducted over 30 short term programs in which over 600 participants from all over the country have been trained. Each program involves a unique and innovative mode of imparting education and training by exposing the students to the state of the art research facilities at CORE-TIET apart from through interactive partnership with the industry and research institutions.
