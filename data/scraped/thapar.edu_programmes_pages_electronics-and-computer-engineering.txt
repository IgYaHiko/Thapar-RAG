Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology
Electronics and Computer Engineering
Electronic, as well as computer engineers are in great demand in industries across the globe. Joint applications such as, mobile phones, space shuttles, desktops, tablets, etc are few of the examples where competence in both of the fields is of paramount importance. The increased use of these technologies in all aspects of our lives ensures continued relevance and its expansion in the future. The Electronics and Computer Engineering undergraduate program is a course which aims to integrate these two separate engineering fields to meet the joint demands made by the electronic and computer industries in today"s world. This program blends various courses of both fields over the period of four years with an emphasis on the design of computing systems.
