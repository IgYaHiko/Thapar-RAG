Dean Academics Notice Board
The Office of the Dean of Academic Affairs (DoAA) is committed to fostering academic excellence and innovation across all programs and disciplines. It oversees curriculum development, academic policies, and student academic progress. The office serves as a bridge between faculty, students, and administration to ensure a dynamic and enriching learning environment. With a focus on continuous improvement, it supports initiatives that enhance teaching, learning, and scholarly engagement.
