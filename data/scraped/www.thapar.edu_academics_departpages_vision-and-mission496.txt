Vision and Mission
Vision
To be recognized as a leader committed to excellence in Computer Science and Engineering education, research and innovation.
Mission
To provide the state of art infrastructure and professional environment that enables students and staff to make significant contribution in Computer Science and Engineering.
To be dynamic, innovative and flexible in devising academic programmes, structures and mechanisms.
To produce globally competent graduates having creative skills and ethical values keeping pace with ever-changing technological advancements in Computer Science and Engineering.
To carry out quality research and development for the benefit of society.
