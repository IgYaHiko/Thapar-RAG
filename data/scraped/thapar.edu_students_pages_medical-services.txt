Health Centre
The Health Centre, TIET serves as a critical provider of basic medical services for both staff and students, functioning as a vital resource within the Institute premises. Equipped with restroom and dressing room facilities, as well as a fully operational dispensary, it is staffed by a dedicated team of medical professionals who prioritize the well-being and safety of all stakeholders.
The Centre is prepared to address a wide range of medical needs, whether they involve sudden illnesses, minor injuries, or the provision of urgent medical attention. This ensures that faculty members and students alike receive and effective medical assistance whenever necessary.
As an essential supplement to the overall welfare of the Institute community, the Health Centre stands ready to serve, emphasizing the importance of safeguarding the health and well-being of all those under its care. It is committed to ensuring that healthcare services are readily accessible and of the highest quality, thereby supporting the Institute's mission to promote a healthy and productive environment for learning and work.
Health Centre, TIET offers comprehensive primary healthcare and emergency medical services to all students, staff, and their dependents. It prioritizes the health and well-being of its community members by providing accessible and efficient healthcare services.Here's a breakdown of the services and staff available.

Goals of the Health Centre:
Enhance Overall Productivity:
Ensure good health for students, teaching staff, and non-teaching staff through basic medical care and counseling.
Raise Health Awareness:
Educate and inform the Thapar community about current and relevant health issues.
Main Focus Areas:
Medical First Aid and Basic Health Care Services:
Provide immediate care for minor illnesses and injuries.
Prompt and Early Referral:
Refer patients to specialized care when necessary for more serious health concerns.
Immediate Transfer of Emergency Cases:
Utilize Institute ambulance services for swift transportation to hospitals in critical situations.
Health Awareness Programs:
Conduct various initiatives to promote health education and awareness within the community.
By focusing on these goals and core areas, the Health Centre, TIET aims to support the well-being and productivity of its community members, ensuring they have access to essential healthcare services and are well-informed about maintaining good health.
