Research
The thematic area of research is Agro and Industrial Biotechnology based on the expertise and the resource base that exists within the Thapar Technology Campus. However the thematic area viz. Industrial Biotechnology and Agro-Biotechnology are further subdivided into specific research area(s).The research primarily in these areas are carried out as sponsored projects from different national government and non-government funding agencies apart from academic and contract research.
