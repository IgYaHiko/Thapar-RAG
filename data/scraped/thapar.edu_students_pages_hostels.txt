Hostels
Hostels:
Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology offers 17 on-campus hostels, including 10 boys’ hostels and 7 girls’ hostels, providing a comfortable home away from home. All hostels are equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, essential amenities, and well-maintained living spaces.Students benefit from a safe and secure environment with 24×7 security and dedicated support staff. The hostels promote a friendly, inclusive atmosphere that supports both academic focus and campus life.

HOSTEL GUEST ROOM BOOKING FACILITY
The Institute provides a
Hostel Guest Room Booking Facility
for the convenience of students and their parents/guardians.
Guest rooms are available
exclusively for female guests
. Male guests are not permitted to stay in the hostel guest rooms.
Guest room accommodation is allotted only on a
genuine need basis
and is subject to availability and approval by the concerned authority.
The facility is intended primarily for
medical needs or emergency situations
and is not meant for routine or personal stays.
Eligible students or their parents/guardians may submit their request through the
dedicated online booking portal
.
Guest rooms can be
booked online
through the Institute's dedicated booking portal.
Accommodation is provided primarily to parents or students based on medical needs or emergency situations.
Guest rooms can now be booked online through our dedicated booking portal.
