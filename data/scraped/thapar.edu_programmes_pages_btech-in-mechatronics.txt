Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology
Mechatronics
The hybrid of electronic, electrical and mechanical engineering, ‘Mechatronics’ is a unique programme at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology that clubs the distinguishing features of each field to yield the best competitive mix for current generation. Mechatronics professionals are the technicians and engineers who design and maintain automated equipment. They work beside engineers and scientists, often assisting in the research and development of production.
