Student Consultative Committee(SCC)
To obtain the students feedback, Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology has a Students Consultative Committee (SCC) comprising of ex-officio faculty representatives and over 200 student representatives from across the various disciplines. SCC's objective is to assist the administration in preparing and implementing students' welfare plans.
SCC meets atleast once a semester.
