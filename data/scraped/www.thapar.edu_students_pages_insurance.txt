Insurance
Amartya Siksha Yojna Insurance Policy
To cover the entire balance expenses towards education in case of accidental death/permanent total disablement of the earning parent during the course of study at TIET.
The students are also insured for rupees 5 lakhs individually in case of accidental death/permanent disablement during the course of study at TIET.
Note: The Insurance Policy will be renewed on 30-09-2026.
