Services Offered
To promote entrepreneurship in Punjab and adjoining states following services are offered especially to small and medium scale industries, NGO’s, students, teachers, managers and entrepreneurs or any individual.
Organizing customized Entrepreneurship Development Programs (EDP’s)
Providing customized reports on enterprise development on specific products, thrust areas or turnkey projects
Infrastructural and incubation support to select Entrepreneurs after having an MoU
Regulatory clearances and strategic interventions for market launch support to Entrepreneur or Technopreneur
Technology Transfer and Intellectual Property Management
Training and Consultancy is provided in setting up of units in IT and Enabled services, Embedded systems, Rapid Prototyping/Manufacturing and Bio Technology.
