Key Responsibility Areas
CTD is responsible for the following:-
Identifying the need for Aptitude & Personality training
Conceptualizing, designing, organizing and implementing training programs based on the above needs
Providing on-campus mentoring and career counselling sessions
Inviting Industry experts to interact with students on contemporary employability and work environment challenges
Liaising with CILP to understand current corporate requirements and accordingly customizing training programs
Test administration and delivery of standardized tests like the GMAT (Pearson Testing) with technical support from Centre for Information & Technology Management.
Apart from having a strong student centric vision & focus as manifested in the activities above, CTD also facilitates the conduct of training programs for staff- both teaching and non-teaching. These programs encompass both technical and behavioural elements of development, ensuring enhanced productivity at work place.
Note:-
CTD also oversees similar functions for the LMT School of Management at Dera Bassi, where it actively liaises with its 'Head- Admissions & Placement' to plan and execute various training programs.
