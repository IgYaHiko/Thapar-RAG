EXECUTIVE PROFILE
Academic and technology leader with three decades of unbroken service at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology (TIET), Patiala — spanning frontline cybersecurity and AI research, university-wide digital infrastructure command as Chief Technology Officer, and institution-level academic governance as Dean, Academic Affairs. Joined TIET in January 1996 and has since built and operated its entire campus network, ERP and cybersecurity backbone; supervises doctoral and Master's research at scale; secures competitive national and international funding (ICMR, Erasmus+/European Union, Tata Consultancy Services, DRDO); and has delivered over 100 invited talks, keynotes and resource-person sessions nationally. As a National Board of Accreditation (NBA) Evaluator, has personally conducted 50+ accreditation evaluations of Tier-1 and Tier-2 engineering institutes across India — direct, external experience in judging institutional quality. Currently leads the HPC/GPU compute infrastructure, as CTO, for TIET's newly announced collaboration with Imperial College London (announced at the India AI Impact Summit, Patiala, April 2026) to co-develop AI4PDEs-based physicsinformed and generative-AI "digital twin" models forecasting air pollution across Northern India — the systemsengineering backbone of one of the university's flagship AI research partnerships. Most recently, has deliberately supplemented deep technical and academic expertise with general-management readiness for institutional leadership — completing an IIM Kozhikode executive programme for Chief Technology Officers, four NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute credentials, and two Kellogg (Northwestern) executive programmes in AI strategy and growth strategy, all across 2024– 2025.
